# Agent SIM ownership-transfer fee — scam warning

**Scam type:** Fake MTN agent (`fake_agent`)
**Status:** Active warning (added 23 November 2026)
**Source:** UMMAAG alert, 5 November

## What agents are being told

MoMo agents are being told to pay a fee to change or transfer ownership of their Agent Business SIM.

## Why it is a scam

The regularisation exercise for Agent Business SIMs has not commenced, and Mobile Money Fintech Ltd does not charge any fee for transfer of ownership. Anyone asking an agent to pay a fee to change or transfer SIM ownership is misrepresenting the process.

## What to do instead

- Do not pay any fee to change or transfer ownership of your Agent Business SIM.
- If you receive a call, message or visit asking for such a fee, report it by dialling **419** from an MTN number (MTN Y'ello Protect).
- Never share your MoMo PIN with anyone.

This entry names no person or number, and no reporter identities are included. It is a pattern warning, so the usual three-reporter flagging rule does not apply to it.
